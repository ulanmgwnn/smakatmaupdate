## Brand & Style

This design system reflects the poise, heritage, and forward-looking rigor of elite global educational institutions. It marries classical academic authority with modern clarity, cultivating an atmosphere of prestige, trust, and aspiration for prospective families, faculty, and alumni.

The aesthetic fuses **Corporate / Modern** precision with **Editorial Elegance**:
- **Authoritative Anchors:** Deep, dense navy elements ground page layouts with institutional heritage and stability.
- **Warm Prestige:** Luminous amber and warm gold accents infuse warmth, achievement, and distinction without sacrificing seriousness.
- **Editorial Legibility:** Expressive, high-contrast serif headlines deliver intellectual grace, counterbalanced by highly systematic sans-serif interfaces for seamless navigation.
- **Balanced Structure:** Symmetrical rhythm, elevated card compositions, and distinct horizontal accent ribbons form a structured, welcoming journey.

## Colors

The color palette leverages a high-contrast triad between deep nocturnal navy, vibrant academic gold, and crisp neutral foundations.

- **Primary (`#0A1D37`)**: The cornerstone of the institutional identity. Applied to high-prominence headers, hero strips, persistent global navigation bars, full-width stat strips, and primary action buttons.
- **Secondary (`#F4A900`)**: The celebratory accent. Reserved for high-intent conversion points ("Apply Now"), award badges, active states, key milestone figures, and focal emphasis text within headlines.
- **Tertiary (`#1E3A8A`)**: A refined collegiate royal blue. Serves as hover states for primary buttons, link focus rings, interactive sub-navigation highlights, and subtle borders on dark surfaces.
- **Neutral (`#1F2937`)**: Slate charcoal for primary text reproduction, ensuring superior legibility and softer optical contrast against pure white than absolute black.
- **Surfaces**: Primary layouts alternate between Pure White (`#FFFFFF`) for elevated cards and crisp content bands, and Light Gray (`#F8F9FA`) for page grounding and subtle architectural grouping. A warm parchment tint (`#FFFBEB`) is selectively applied to admission and achievement callouts.

## Typography

The type system balances humanistic tradition with digital precision.

- **Headline Hierarchy**: Set in **Playfair Display**. Headings frequently use intentional two-line chromatic pairing: line one rendered in deep authoritative Navy (`#0A1D37`) and line two rendered in Gold (`#F4A900`) to direct eye movement and convey institutional warmth.
- **Body & Microcopy**: Set in **Inter** to ensure maximum legibility across dense course catalogs, admission schedules, and metric callouts.
- **Eyebrow Tags**: Rendered in uppercase **Inter Bold** with `letterSpacing: 0.12em` in Gold (`#F4A900`) to clearly label sections prior to main editorial headlines.
- **Numerical Stats**: Set in Playfair Display with tabular lining when presented in horizontal ribbons, celebrating institutional metrics (e.g., student counts, pass rates) with elegance.

## Layout & Spacing

This design system uses a strict **12-column fixed grid** bounded by a maximum content container of `1280px`, complemented by edge-to-edge structural anchor bands.

- **Vertical Cadence**: Standard section separation follows `space-4xl` (96px) on desktop and `space-2xl` (48px) on mobile screens. Micro-grouping between eyebrow labels, headlines, and descriptions uses tighter `space-xs` through `space-md` multiples.
- **Structural Bands**: Full-bleed ribbons (`#0A1D37`) cut across the neutral page canvas to house metric indicators and school pillar summaries, anchoring the viewport with institutional authority.
- **Layered Overlaps**: Hero cards, quick-stat panels, and thematic badges employ negative vertical positioning (offset `-2rem` to `-3rem`) overlapping section transitions to generate dimensionality and physical depth.
- **Breakpoints**:
  - **Desktop (1024px+)**: 12 columns, 32px gutters, multi-column program and stat distribution.
  - **Tablet (768px - 1023px)**: 8 columns, 24px gutters, program cards flow into 2-column matrices.
  - **Mobile (< 767px)**: 4 columns, 16px gutters, stat strips reflow into 2x2 grids, and overlapping badges anchor statically beneath imagery.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy combines clean architectural layers with luminous, crisp ambient shadows to preserve an organized academic ambiance.

- **Level 0 (Flat Surface)**: Pure White (`#FFFFFF`) or Light Gray (`#F8F9FA`) background surfaces without elevation.
- **Level 1 (Subtle Cards & Lists)**: `0px 2px 8px rgba(10, 29, 55, 0.04), 0px 1px 2px rgba(10, 29, 55, 0.02)` paired with a soft `1px solid #E5E7EB` outline. Used for academic program cards, horizontal list rows, and directory items.
- **Level 2 (Overlapping Badges & Interactive Panels)**: `0px 12px 24px -4px rgba(10, 29, 55, 0.08), 0px 4px 8px -2px rgba(10, 29, 55, 0.04)`. Applied to floating achievement badges (e.g., "A Legacy of Excellence"), sticky headers, and quick-inquiry modules.
- **Level 3 (Modal Dialogs & Mega Menus)**: `0px 24px 48px -12px rgba(10, 29, 55, 0.16)`. Provides focused separation for admission forms, campus tour modals, and deep-level navigation panels.
- **Hover Transitions**: Elevated cards shift upward by `4px` accompanied by a shadow shift to `0px 16px 32px -4px rgba(10, 29, 55, 0.12)`, reinforcing tactile quality without jarring distraction.

## Shapes

The shape system adopts a **Soft (`roundedness: 1`)** standard. This restraint maintains institutional solemnity and architectural discipline, avoiding informal or overly playful curves.

- **Cards & Visual Containers**: Bound by `0.5rem` (8px) radii, giving a structured, crisp look with softened corners.
- **Interactive Controls (Buttons, Inputs, Selects)**: Defined with `0.375rem` (6px) radii to provide reliable, professional affordances.
- **Ribbon & Stat Containers**: Pill containers or subtle `0.5rem` rounded modules set within navy ribbons.
- **Badges & Crests**: Achievement medallions and micro-badges use circular frames (`rounded-full`) or soft shield-like chamfers to reinforce institutional heritage.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary CTA ("Apply Now")**: Vibrant Amber background (`#F4A900`), Charcoal or Deep Navy text (`#0A1D37`), bold weight, subtle forward arrow icon on the right. Hover transitions to `#E09B00`.
- **Secondary Authority ("Discover Our School")**: Deep Navy background (`#0A1D37`), White text (`#FFFFFF`). Hover transitions to `#1E3A8A`.
- **Outline / Video Action**: Transparent background with 1.5px border in Navy (`#0A1D37`) or White (`#FFFFFF`) depending on the surface tier, accompanied by an inline directional glyph or play icon.

### Cards
- **Program & Pathway Cards**: Vertical format with a `16:10` photographic header, `0.5rem` radius, 1px neutral border (`#E5E7EB`), pure white background, and Level 1 elevation. Metadata tags (e.g., "Grades 9–12") appear at the base rendered in bold gold typography.
- **Quick Fact Cards**: Compact white blocks featuring top-aligned slate or gold icons, an assertive stat value in Playfair Display, and a concise label in Inter.

### Badges & Medallions
- **Excellence Badge**: White card surface with soft shadow, featuring a gold ribbon/rosette icon, bold headline ("Legacy of Excellence"), and secondary supporting copy. Overlaps photography margins to ground visual focal points.
- **Status Chips**: Upper-case 11px chips with subtle amber-tinted background (`#FEF3C7`) and dark amber typography (`#92400E`) for program categorization and grade tiers.

### Form Inputs & Controls
- **Input Fields**: Crisp 1px border (`#D1D5DB`) over white background, `0.375rem` border radius, padding `0.75rem 1rem`. Focus state triggers a 2px primary ring (`#0A1D37`).
- **Checkboxes & Radios**: 18px square/round controls in `#0A1D37` when active, with high-contrast white indicators.

### Horizontal Stat Strip
- Deep Navy container (`#0A1D37`) displaying modular metric points separated by subtle navy borders (`#1E3A8A`). Each item integrates a fine line-art icon in gold or white, a bold serif metric, and a concise two-line descriptor.
